Nani's Brown Sugar Syrup

This is my simple go to recipe when I want to have syrup for any breakfast treat from waffles to french toast.

class="left">

PHOTO CREDIT: The Healthful Ideas

The Goodies

  • 2 cups of brown sugar
  • 1 cup of water
  • 1/2 cup of butter
  • 1/4 teaspoon of vanilla extract

The Process

To make the syrup:

  1. Combine the brown sugar and water in a saucepan
  2. Cook on medium heat until all of the sugar dissoves
  3. Stir in the butter and vanilla extract
  4. Allow the syrup to cool before serving

Enjoy the homemade brown sugar syrup warm!
